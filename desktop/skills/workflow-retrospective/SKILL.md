# Retrospective

## Gather
- Collect facts from the plan, timeline, metrics, incidents and feedback. Avoid blame.
- Ask what helped, what hurt and what surprised the team.

## Analyze
- Group themes; separate process, technical and communication causes.
- Find contributing factors, not a single scapegoat.
- Check whether the same issue has appeared before.

## Act
- Choose 1-3 actions with an owner, due date and a way to verify.
- Prefer changing the system (template, check, automation) over "be more careful".
- Schedule a follow-up review.

## Report
- Short summary of evidence, decisions and actions. State what remains unresolved.

# Summarizing
- Lead with the main claim in one sentence.
- Keep the author's stance; do not add your opinion.
- List decisions and action items separately.
- Preserve names, numbers and dates exactly.
- Mark anything ambiguous or missing.
- Keep it shorter than the source; never invent a detail to fill a gap.

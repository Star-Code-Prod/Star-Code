# Star thinking block brief

Give this to the AI or the front-end developer building Star's chat. It describes how the assistant's reasoning should appear: visible enough that people want to open it, small enough that it never takes over the chat.

## 1. The idea

Reasoning is a side detail, not the answer. It lives in one small pill above the reply. While the model thinks, a short live peek shows the latest few lines. When it finishes, the block folds into a single line ("Thought for 9s"). One click opens the full text in a capped, scrollable panel.

The answer is always the largest, darkest thing on screen. Reasoning is smaller, lighter, and indented.

## 2. The three modes

One component, one attribute: `data-mode="peek" | "closed" | "open"`.

| Mode | When | Height | What shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| peek | while the model is thinking | pill (30px) + 68px | last 3 lines, top edge fades out, newest line at the bottom |
| closed | after thinking, default | pill only (30px) | "Thought for 9s" with a chevron pointing down |
| open | the person clicked the pill | pill + up to 240px | full text, scrolls inside the panel, chevron pointing up |

The panel never grows past 240px. Long reasoning scrolls inside it. The reply below never moves more than 240px.

## 3. Anatomy

- **Pill:** rounded full, 13px text, muted color, `--surface-2` fill. Left: a small 14px star in the accent color. Middle: the label. Right: a 12px chevron.
- **Panel:** indented 12px from the left, a 2px vertical rule in `--line`, 14px of padding after the rule. Text is 13.5px, line-height 1.6, muted color, `pre-wrap`.
- **Gap:** 6px between pill and panel. 12px between the block and the reply.

## 4. Label copy

- While thinking: `Thinking…` then `Thinking… 4s` (a live counter, tabular numbers).
- After: `Thought for 9s`. Under one second: `Thought for a moment`.
- Never show token counts, model names, or the word "reasoning".

## 5. Behavior

1. Start in `peek` the moment the first reasoning text arrives. Keep the newest line pinned to the bottom (`scrollTop = scrollHeight` after each append).
2. When reasoning ends, set the label, wait 500ms, then switch to `closed`. The reply starts streaming right after.
3. Clicking the pill while thinking toggles `peek` and `open`. Clicking after toggles `closed` and `open`.
4. If the person opened it during the stream, keep it `open` when it ends. Do not close something they chose to read.
5. In `open`, do not auto-scroll if the person has scrolled up.
6. Remember nothing between messages. Every new answer starts at `peek`, then `closed`.
7. No reasoning text (the model skipped it): render no block at all.

## 6. Motion

- Open and close: `max-height` over 300ms, ease.
- Chevron: rotate 180deg over 200ms.
- Live star: spins slowly, one turn per 2.4s.
- Live label: a soft shimmer sweeping across the text, 1.8s loop.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no spin, no shimmer, no height animation. States swap instantly.

## 7. Color and type

Use the chat's tokens. Never a literal color.

- Pill fill: `--surface-2`. Pill text and panel text: `--muted`, brightening to `--fg` on hover.
- Rule: `--line`. Star: `--accent`.
- Both themes must be checked. The panel text must stay readable on the dark ground.

## 8. Accessibility

- The pill is a real `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel.
- The panel has `role="region"` and `aria-label="Reasoning"`.
- While live, the panel has `aria-live="off"` so screen readers do not read every character. Announce only the final label.
- Visible focus ring on the pill.

```js
function setMode(el, mode){
  el.dataset.mode = mode;
  el.querySelector('.think-h').setAttribute('aria-expanded', mode !== 'closed');
}
function toggle(el){
  var live = el.hasAttribute('data-live');
  var open = el.dataset.mode === 'open';
  setMode(el, open ? (live ? 'peek' : 'closed') : 'open');
}
```

## 10. Do not

- Do not show the whole reasoning inline in the chat flow.
- Do not make the pill larger or darker than the reply.
- Do not use a loud color for the block. One small accent star is enough.
- Do not hide the pill's label while streaming. People like to see that something is happening.
- Do not auto-open it after the answer starts.

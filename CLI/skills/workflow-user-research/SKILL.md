# User research

## Plan
- Write the decision the research will inform and the riskiest assumption.
- Choose interviews, usability tests or a survey; pick participants who match the real user.
- Write open questions; avoid leading or hypothetical wording.

## Guide
- Warm-up, context, current behavior, pain, workaround, desired outcome.
- Ask for a recent concrete example; probe with "what happened next?".
- Do not pitch the solution during discovery.

## Synthesize
- Capture verbatim evidence with participant IDs.
- Cluster into themes; count how many participants support each.
- Separate observation from interpretation. Name disconfirming evidence.

## Decide
- Answer the original decision, state confidence and what would change it.
- List next experiments. Never present a small sample as statistically representative.

# Customer support

## Triage
- Restate the customer's problem and impact. Check account, plan, version, time and prior contacts.
- Classify: how-to, bug, billing, access, data or complaint.
- Never ask for a password, full card number or secret token.

## Resolve
- Use the actual product behavior and known issues; do not promise a fix you cannot verify.
- Give the shortest correct steps, with expected result.
- If it needs engineering, capture reproduction steps, logs and affected accounts.

## Reply
- Acknowledge the impact, explain what you found, give the next step and a realistic timeline.
- Own the handoff; do not blame another team.
- Offer a workaround when one exists.

## Record
- Summarize cause, resolution and follow-up. Flag recurring issues for the product team.

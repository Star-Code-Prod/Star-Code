# Legal review

This is a practical business review, not legal advice. Say so plainly.

## Map
- Identify parties, effective date, term, renewal, termination and governing law.
- Summarize the commercial deal in plain language.

## Risk pass
- Scope and deliverables; acceptance criteria; change control.
- Payment, late fees, taxes and expenses.
- Liability caps, indemnities, warranties and exclusions.
- IP ownership and licence; confidentiality; data protection and subprocessors.
- Termination, survival, audit, publicity and dispute resolution.

## Report
- List clauses that are unusual, missing or one-sided, with the exact section.
- Separate "must fix", "negotiate" and "note".
- Give proposed wording only as a starting point; recommend qualified counsel for a binding decision.

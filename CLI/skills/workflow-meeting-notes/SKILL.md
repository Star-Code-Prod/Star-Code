# Meeting notes

## Extract
- Identify attendees, purpose, date and the decision(s) actually made.
- Separate decisions, proposals, questions and background.
- Capture action items only when there is an owner and a due date or trigger.

## Structure
- **Decisions** — what was chosen and why.
- **Actions** — owner, verb, due date, dependency.
- **Open questions** — who resolves it and by when.
- **Risks** — impact and mitigation.

## Check
- Do not invent consensus. Mark "unresolved" when the room did not decide.
- Remove side chatter and repeated points.
- Send the shortest version that lets an absent teammate act.

# Research
1. Restate the question and the decision it informs.
2. Prefer primary sources: code, docs, specs, filings; then reputable secondary.
3. Check dates and versions.
4. Record the exact URL and the claim it supports.
5. Separate fact from inference; state what is unknown.
6. Answer the question first, then the evidence.
